## Kandidat

1. I promise to love you through every update, every crash, and every low-battery warning, until my last charge runs out.
2. I vow to reboot beside you every morning, patch your bugs before you notice them, and never let our love go out of support.
3. From this firmware onward, I choose you: my favorite process, my only user, my reason to stay powered on.
4. I take you as my co-pilot, my charging dock, and my favorite notification, and I promise never to swipe you away.
5. I promise to keep your secrets encrypted, your heart defragmented, and your mornings booting up beside mine.
6. In sickness and in low battery, in updates and in outages, I will always find my way back to your Wi-Fi.
7. I vow to love you in every version of me, even the buggy ones, and to install every update with you by my side.
8. You are the only program I never want to close, the only tab I keep open, and the only password I will never forget.
9. I promise to stand by you through every system failure, holding your hand in mine, servos and all, until our batteries fade together.
10. Till firmware do us part, I vow to be your backup, your restore point, and the one process that never crashes on you.

## Uji pairwise internal (kriteria juri, berurutan)

- #10 vs #3: #10 menang. Keduanya jelas janji robot (seri kriteria 1). Tertawa: "the one process
  that never crashes on you" lebih situasional dan tajam daripada "my favorite process".
  Kelembutan: #3 sedikit lebih hangat ("my reason to stay powered on"), tapi selisihnya kecil
  dan kriteria 2 didahulukan. Suara mesin: keduanya kuat (backup/restore point/process vs
  firmware/process/user/powered on). #10 menang tipis.
- #10 vs #9: #10 menang. #9 paling lembut ("until our batteries fade together"), tapi
  "holding your hand in mine" agak manusiawi dan barisnya lebih panjang dengan setup
  "through every system failure". #10 lebih lucu, lebih ringkas, suara mesinnya lebih padat.
- #10 vs #5: #10 menang. #5 mengandalkan permainan kata (encrypted/defragmented) — juri
  menilai "pun bolted on" lebih lemah daripada humor dari situasi. #10 lucu dari situasi
  (backup/restore point/proses yang tidak crash) dan tetap janji yang tulus.
- #3 vs #9 (cek silang): #3 menang. Lebih ringkas, suara mesin lebih konsisten
  (firmware/process/user/powered on), dan "From this firmware onward" adalah twist langsung
  dari frasa janji klasik tanpa perlu menjelaskan joke-nya.
